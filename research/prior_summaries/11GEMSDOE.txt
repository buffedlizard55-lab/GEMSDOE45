TWO BUGS WE CAUGHT IN OUR OWN CODE Both would have produced a beautiful lie. Emitting the answer key The first emission sweep added the full label raster to the prediction. Inside a scored block, that is the held-out truth, so every arm scored a perfect 1.0000 and the trace-inpainting arm appeared to win by +0.19. In the contest the known faults are masked and the unknown ones are the target, so the honest analogue is to emit only the known catalogue. Charging the whole map The fast scorer did not zero predictions outside the scored region, so three quarters of every prediction was billed as false positive. Every number read about ten times too low. Both fixes have regression tests, and the leakage gate that caught the first one is now a hard abort in the experiment runner.
