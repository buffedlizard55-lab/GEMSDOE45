Review the preregistered candidates, source ledger, and full results → Round-5: The Defect Was the Emission Policy, Not the Features Reproduction first. scripts/cache_fold_models.py re-trained raw19 / multi25 / multi30 / random02 on the frozen masked protocol and matched evidence/holdout_masked.json on every fold of every arm to full float64 precision (multi25 = 0.09071277311870418). Rounds 1–4 are verified reproducible. That verification exposed the real defect: on the same protocol the uniform random 2 % control scores 0.13788 — better than every learned arm we have built. The official metric gives a truth pixel credit if any predicted pixel falls within 300 m (3 px) of it, and takes max over that 5 × 5 cell. Two predictions inside one cell therefore add false-positive mass and add zero true-positive credit. Every round 1–4 arm — and the archived 06ca61e5 artifact — emitted top- k by probability, which piles mass exactly where it is wasted. The sparsest emission that can still credit every truth pixel is one with no two predictions in a 5 × 5 block: non-maximum suppression at radius 2, density 1/25. Why H32 should catch faults missing from USGS/INGENIOUS: a 1–10 m fault scarp is a step over 20–100 m — sub-pixel on the 100 m grid but a resolved one-sided gradient ridge at 10 m. Null hypotheses: shorelines of pluvial Lake Lahontan, terraces, roads and canal banks are also 1–10 m steps — the holdout gain is measured on catalogue traces, not on the hidden expert truth.
