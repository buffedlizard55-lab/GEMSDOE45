Zero-outside diagnostic TIFFs and their non-conformant validation records remain unlinked in docs/downloads/ for auditability. The mechanism, from the published metric algebra. Because TP w takes a max over each truth pixel's 300 m neighbourhood, extra dots near an already-covered truth pixel add no TP but do add FP w . Substituting FN w = G − TP w into the published formula collapses it to DTI = TP w / (0.8·G + 0.2·(TP w + FP w )) — verified numerically to 1e-9 against src/gemsdoe30/metric.py on both dotted artifacts. Differentiating gives a clean break-even: a dot is worth adding iff its marginal TP credit per unit FP cost exceeds 0.2 × DTI (≈0.052 at DTI 0.26). Redundant dots have marginal TP = 0 and are therefore strictly harmful. That predicts exactly the observed ordering. Portal score rises monotonically as dots are de-clumped: 121,131 dots at 100 m spacing → 0.1922; 60,069 at 200 m → 0.2477; 44,090 at 282.8–300 m → 0.2600. The winning minimum separation lands on the 300 m kernel radius itself — the scale at which two dots stop competing for the same truth pixels. This is the same effect this repo measured independently as the 6.38× Poisson-thinning multiplier ( IR-30-033 ). So the remaining lever is evidence, not thinning. d2.8 occupies only 0.85 % of the footprint; a 300 m hex packing could hold roughly 15× more dots. The file is evidence-limited, not packing-limited. Beating 0.2600 requires more non-redundant dots placed where un-catalogued faults actually are — i.e. a better score field over the 19 GeoDAWN bands — not a different emission schedule.
