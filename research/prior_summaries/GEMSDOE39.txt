Irregularities flagged for review ID Finding Disposition IR-40-01 Off-catalogue fault density is measured at 3.3–4.4× inside 200 m of a mapped trace, while the one controlled live experiment in this family gained +0.0070 by removing dots within 200 m. My earlier claim that these two cannot both be right was wrong. **Corrected 2026-10-05 — my earlier framing was wrong.** I wrote that the measured profile (3.3–4.4× off-catalogue density inside 200 m) and the one controlled live experiment (+0.0070 from removing dots within 200 m) "cannot both be right". They can. Under DTI = TP/(0.2TP + 0.2n + 0.8K), removing dots always improves the score whenever each removed dot’s marginal credit falls below 0.2×DTI (≈ 0.0556 at DTI = 0.2778). So +0.0070 shows only that those 2,545 dots earned less than the bar — not that the band contains no truth. Decisively, DrivenData staff confirmed in thread #11516 post 4 that the mask is pixel-exact (identical to the training labels, no halo), that a prediction near a known trace but far from new-fault truth is fully penalised , and that “a new-fault ground truth pixel can indeed lie within 300 m of a known fault trace… identifying these corrections is one outcome we are aiming for”. We still shipped the 200 m exclusion, because the pre-declared SPRT was run on that exact field and changing it afterwards would be post-hoc selection. But it is now an open risk, not a settled choice : the official statement argues for emitting there. Neither local instrument can adjudicate it — instrument B’s surrogate (off-catalogue SGMC) is enriched beside the catalogue by construction, and instrument A’s truth is the catalogue, so both are circular on this question. **Testing it is the highest-value use of the next submission slot.** IR-40-02 The previous revision of this page claimed the uniqueness gate ran against 241 retrievable rasters.
