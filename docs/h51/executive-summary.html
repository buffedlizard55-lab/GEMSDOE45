Short note on what makes this one different

It is not a copy and it is not a re-render of an earlier raster: the predicted pixel set is new, built from the catalogue's own survival curve. Concretely — (1) the extension beyond each mapped tip is drawn per fault from a Kaplan–Meier product-limit curve fitted to the catalogue's own relay gaps, so it varies tip to tip (30 distinct realised lengths, asserted in the build and in the tests); (2) splay/parallel-strand positions inside the same fault zone are candidates, matching the organizers' published definition of a new fault; (3) the dots are chosen by greedy maximum expected coverage of the leaked truth density under the official 300 m triangular kernel and stopped at the metric's own marginal bar. What it is not is validated: no instrument available here ranks it against the hidden new-fault truth.
