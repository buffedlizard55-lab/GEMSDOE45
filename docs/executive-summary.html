How submission works, once approved

  1. Confirm the promotion gate has passed; verify the exact filename and hash. A local format pass alone is insufficient.
  2. Download the .tif, or the ZIP containing just that TIFF. Do not upload this report, a JSON receipt, or a PNG overview.
  3. Go to the official competition. Sign into your registered account and check eligibility and rules. This site cannot accept those legal commitments on your behalf.
  4. Open New submission. Under File to submit, choose the downloaded TIFF or ZIP.
  5. Paste the short note above in Note (optional). The artifact’s unique filename also identifies the experiment.
  6. Submit only if the slot is authorized. Record the organizer receipt, score and filename in the score register. Do not infer a score from a local test.
  7. Before the deadline choose one final submission for both prize rounds. The rules state three submissions per week; check the current platform limits and deadline.

Why this file avoids invalid stored values

Every stored cell is finite, between 0 and 1. There is no negative nodata sentinel. Cells outside the template footprint store zero and carry an internal invalid-data mask. The file has one float32 band, 3,730 rows × 3,292 columns, EPSG:32611, 100 m pixels and the template’s exact geotransform.

The published format describes null outside the bounds. Our internal mask preserves null-area semantics without storing NaNs. The platform’s actual mask handling is not verified; we cannot guarantee acceptance without an organizer receipt. We do not silently clip corrupt predictions to conceal a pipeline error.
