What is held out?

Each quadrant is tested once. The next quadrant calibrates an emission fraction from {0.4%, 0.8%, 1.6%}; the other two fit the classifier. Train/calibration/test domains are at least 6 km apart across the center splits. No label-distance, coordinate, previous-prediction, or example-submission-value feature is used.

All four calibration rounds selected the largest candidate budget, 1.6%. This may indicate the search was budget-limited, but increasing the budget after seeing the test results would be a new experiment. Final training uses the median calibration-selected fraction, not an outer-test-selected setting.

What remains unverified?

The missing-from-catalogue discovery target is not observable in these labels. There was no pre-existing holdout in the empty checkout. Comparing the catalogue-pruned H33 artifact against these same labels would be unfair. Therefore we cannot claim to have beaten the earlier group's true holdout best, or its 0.2778 organizer score.

The trained full-footprint artifact is not the out-of-fold prediction map. Full-data inference is allowed to learn from all provided public training labels. The reported holdout numbers come only from the separate fits described above.

Format and uniqueness

The downloaded bytes are re-read independently. The format receipt checks grid, dtype, band count, finite range, outside-footprint values and mask. Our pixel identity comparison normalizes only the outside footprint: NaN versus zero packaging cannot masquerade as a unique prediction. Invalid in-footprint values are counted, not repaired in the prior audit.
